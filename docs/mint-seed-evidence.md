# Mint seed evidence for issue 79

The initial catalogue links two Italian Coins to the Italian state mint in Rome:

| Coin                                                        | Issuer | Mint production evidence                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 50th Anniversary of the World Food Programme                | Italy  | [Italian Mint 2002–2011 catalogue](https://www.shop.ipzs.it/pub/media/wysiwyg/catalogo/dieci_anni_euro.pdf) lists and specifies this 2004 coin. |
| 1st Anniversary of the Signing of the European Constitution | Italy  | [Italian Mint 2002–2011 catalogue](https://www.shop.ipzs.it/pub/media/wysiwyg/catalogo/dieci_anni_euro.pdf) lists and specifies this 2005 coin. |

The [Italian Mint's national coin analysis centre](https://www.ipzs.it/it/soluzioni/numismatica-medaglie-monetazione/cnac/) identifies its physical mint workshop at Via Gino Capponi 47/49 in Rome. The [Italian Mint facilities page](https://www.ipzs.it/chi-siamo/azienda/stabilimenti) confirms the Rome Mint is operating. Exact beginning year for this facility is left unknown. Other Coins remain unassigned until their production site is verified for the particular issue.
